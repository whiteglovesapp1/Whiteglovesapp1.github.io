White Gloves

Your personal fitness companion — curated workout videos to help you stay healthy and keep moving.

What it does

White Gloves helps you find and stay consistent with workouts by curating and recommending fitness videos from YouTube, tailored to your goals — whether that's strength training, cardio, yoga, or general wellness.

How it works

White Gloves uses the YouTube Data API to search and surface high-quality workout content from YouTube's public video library, so you spend less time scrolling and more time exercising.

Your data, your control

White Gloves only accesses the YouTube data needed to recommend videos to you, and never posts, modifies, or acts on your YouTube account. See our Privacy Policy for full details on data handling.
