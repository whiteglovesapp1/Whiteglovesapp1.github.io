Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

White Gloves ("we," "our," or "us") is a fitness app that curates and recommends workout videos to help you stay healthy and active. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use it, and the choices you have. By connecting your Google account to White Gloves, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Access

When you sign in with Google and authorize White Gloves, we request access to the following through the YouTube Data API:

  • Public YouTube video and channel data (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, categories) used to search for and recommend workout content
  • Your video watch/search preferences within the app, used to improve recommendations
  • Basic Google account profile information (name, email address, profile photo) used for sign-in and identification

We do not access your private messages, payment information, or any Google data outside the scopes you explicitly approve during sign-in.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To search YouTube's public video library and recommend workout videos suited to your fitness interests
  • To personalize your in-app experience based on the types of workouts you engage with
  • To authenticate you and keep your session secure

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Retention

White Gloves retrieves your YouTube and account data live from Google's servers each time you use the app. We do not maintain a persistent copy of your video or channel data on our own servers. Authentication tokens are stored only as long as needed to keep you signed in, and are deleted when you disconnect your account or revoke access.

4. YouTube API Services Disclosure

White Gloves uses the YouTube API Services. By using White Gloves, you are also agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

For information about how Google collects and processes data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Revoking Access

You can revoke White Gloves' access to your Google account at any time via the Google Security Settings page. Revoking access immediately stops White Gloves from retrieving any further data on your behalf.

6. Data Deletion

Because we do not retain a copy of your YouTube data, revoking access as described above effectively removes our ability to access or process any of your information going forward. If you have questions about any residual data, contact us using the information below.

7. Children's Privacy

White Gloves is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at Kamagamboomondi@gmail.com.
